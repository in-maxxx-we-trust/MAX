Common Questions

Let's Address the Elephant
in the Server Room

We know you have concerns. Here are the honest answers to questions every CTO asks before making the switch.

"This sounds too good to be true. What's the catch?" +
Fair question. The "catch" is that we're software, not hardware. We can't magically make slow disks faster – we architect around them. We use your existing compute's RAM and NVMe as a massive distributed cache. We predict what data you'll need next. We eliminate redundant I/O. It's not magic, it's smart engineering. And yes, it really works – MLCommons verified our benchmarks independently.
"What happens when MAXCLOUD goes down?" +
Your data is still on your storage – we're just an acceleration layer. If MAXCLOUD services stop, you fall back to direct storage access. Yes, it'll be slower, but you won't lose data or access. That said, we maintain 99.999% uptime (5 minutes/year). Our distributed architecture means no single point of failure. Node failures are transparent. Even our control plane can go down without affecting data access.
"How do you handle data consistency?" +
POSIX-compliant strong consistency by default. We use distributed consensus (Raft) for metadata operations. Writes are acknowledged only after durability is guaranteed. For ML workloads that can tolerate eventual consistency, you can tune for even higher performance. Everything is configurable per-volume or per-file.
"What about vendor lock-in?" +
Zero lock-in. Your data stays in your storage, in standard formats. No proprietary data structures. Standard POSIX interface means your code doesn't change. You can remove MAXCLOUD anytime and everything keeps working (just slower). We earn your business every month by delivering value, not by holding your data hostage.
"How does pricing actually work at scale?" +
Simple: you pay for logical capacity used, not physical. 10TB dataset = 10TB pricing, regardless of replication or caching. Volume discounts kick in automatically: 50% off after 1PB, 70% off after 10PB. No charges for I/O operations, snapshots, or bandwidth. Most customers see 60-80% cost reduction versus traditional storage because you need less capacity when your GPUs aren't waiting.
"What if our workload is different?" +
Our ML-driven optimizer adapts to any access pattern. Sequential reads for training? We'll prefetch aggressively. Random access for embeddings? We'll maximize cache hit rates. Mixed workloads? We'll partition resources optimally. The beauty is you don't configure anything – MAXCLOUD learns and adapts automatically. If we can't deliver the performance you need, we'll tell you upfront.
