Yes. MAX processes data on-device with end-to-end encryption. We never see your personal information. You can export or delete all data anytime. We're SOC2, HIPAA, and GDPR compliant.
Can MAX access my bank accounts?
Only with your explicit permission. MAX uses read-only access by default. Transaction capabilities require additional authentication and can be limited to specific actions.
